Simply Cribbage

Change Log

Last updated: October 26, 2026 · App version 2.3.76
The short version Every release of Simply Cribbage, newest first, with what actually changed — not just marketing copy. Most releases are small fixes and polish; that's normal for an actively-maintained app.

2.3.76 Stats now follows Prefs during a hand

October 26, 2026
  • Stats now follows Prefs: both are off from the moment you discard until the hand has been counted, so neither can open in the middle of a hand
  • General improvements and minor bug fixes

2.3.75 A simpler New Game screen, a pegging fix, and clearer Coaching

October 26, 2026
  • The New Game screen is simpler for now: the opening definition stays, and the rotating fun facts, including the USS Wahoo story, are paused
  • Prefs is now off while a hand is in play, and Stats while the game counts, so a settings sheet can no longer end up behind the Count Your Hand box
  • Pegging: a very quick tap right after the computer plays can no longer put the turn out of step
  • Coaching on iPad: the pegging advice says how likely the computer is to hold a scoring reply, the discard advice describes your two cards, and opening Detail before you count gets a coach note
  • How to Play now says a crib flush needs all five cards (the iPad coach's crib flush hint agrees) and that Reduce Motion skips the confetti
  • General improvements and minor bug fixes

2.3.58 A perfect 29 gets a celebration, and the launch no longer jiggles

October 15, 2026
  • A perfect 29 hand is now a moment: confetti, applause, a gold “29 Points!” and a note on the USS Wahoo’s lucky 29, with a button that copies a link so you can read the story in your browser. It works for your hand and your crib
  • In Self Score you earn the celebration by entering 29. If you had a 29 but miscounted it, you get a gentle note instead. When the computer holds a 29 it gives a nod in its own voice (no confetti)
  • The 29 looks the same whether the app counts it or you do: the same size, colour and spacing on all four count screens
  • Self Score miscount messages are gentler for every hand, with a softer colour and calmer wording (“Not quite — you said 24, it’s 29 Points”)
  • The launch no longer jiggles: the logo stays put when the app opens (it used to jump on every iPhone and iPad), and on narrower phones the first screen no longer slides into place

2.3.57 Raised cards stay clear of the board, and small screens fit better

October 14, 2026
  • A card you lift (by tapping it, by Hint, or by dragging it) no longer climbs over the cribbage board on shorter screens and in small iPad windows. The lift is capped to the room there is, and the hand sits a little lower, so there is a little more gap between it and the board
  • Phones and small windows: the discard prompt (“Click 2 cards to discard to the crib…”) was cut off in the one-line message strip. It now picks the longest wording that fits and switches live if the window is resized. The cut for first deal reads on two lines (“Cut: Alex drew A, Noddy drew K”, then “Alex deals first (low card)”) instead of one truncated sentence
  • iPad: the “Dealer · Crib” label was clipped on the score card in small, zoomed windows. It now scales to fit
  • iPhone SE and other very small phones: the trophy (or the Joker card after a loss) on the game-over screen was cut off by the header. The screen now starts below the header and tightens its spacing only as much as it needs to. On the SE, where the board is hidden, the game-over screen also comes up about a second after you peg out instead of waiting for a board animation that is not there
  • Double skunk: the loss jingle on the game-over screen played twice. It now plays once; the two sprays are unchanged
