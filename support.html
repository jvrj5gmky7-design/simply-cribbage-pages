Frequently Asked Questions

The on-screen keyboard doesn't appear when I try to enter a name

This almost always means a third-party keyboard (Grammarly, Gboard, SwiftKey, etc.) is set as your default and is failing to load — a known iOS limitation where apps have no ability to select or override the active keyboard. Tap the globe key to switch to Apple's own keyboard, or check Settings → General → Keyboard. If nothing's typed after a few seconds, the game will continue on its own with a default name.

Is my data private? Does the app track me?

Yes, it's private — the app makes zero network requests, has no accounts or analytics, and everything stays on your device. See the Privacy Policy for the full detail.

How do I reset my stats?

Open Stats from the in-game controls, then use the reset option there. You can reset just your own stats, another player's, or everyone's at once.

Face ID / Touch ID isn't unlocking the app

App Lock relies entirely on iOS's own Face ID / Touch ID system — the app never handles biometrics directly. If unlocking fails, check Settings → Face ID & Passcode (or Touch ID & Passcode) to confirm Simply Cribbage has permission, and that Face ID / Touch ID is working elsewhere on your device.

I found a bug, or have a suggestion

Please reach out at the address above — include the app version (found on the New Game screen or in About, e.g. 2.2.96) and a quick description of what happened. Screenshots help a lot.
